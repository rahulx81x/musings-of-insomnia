# PROLOGUE: THE GENESIS

---

### Where Do I Start Again?

So, where do I start again?  
From the time we first met,  
When formalities marked their existence,  
When talks comprised of short sentences,  
When a feeble smile was let out upon seeing.  

From the time we knew each other better,  
When glances forced a genuine smile,  
When hesitation started to wear off,  
When thoughts were shared and choices were stated.  

From the time we were close,  
When secrets were being revealed,  
When reasons were not required to talk,  
When lives started to spiral together.  

From the time we were one,  
When things were ours, not mine, not yours,  
When the stars witnessed our confessions,  
When the cupid envied our affection.  

From the time cracks emerged,  
When shouts and fights prolonged,  
When vows and ties broke,  
When the sweet little heaven choked.  

From the time we were apart,  
When happiness evaded itself,  
When the tears prickled down every now and then,  
When the realisation of the loss stepped in.  

So, where do I start again?

# ACT I: THE RUPTURE & THE GEOMETRY OF SEPARATION

> *Before the world teaches you how to walk alone, it teaches you the cruelty of geometry. You discover that two lines can travel side by side through every season, share the same sky, and still never touch.*

---

### I. The Cycle of Solitudes

Spring songs summon my soul  
Decorate my deeds with delusion  
Still these summer solitudes  
Inhabit my inner illusion  

The reigning rains run down  
through my thoughts of the traitor  
Cascades of chaos cry  
At the aisle of autumn air  

I wait for the winter warmth  
To soothe my self of sorrows  
To break barriers of the blues  
And take me to my tomorrow!  

---

### II. Parallel Lines

We walk parallel to each other,  
to the world a never-ending path  
with crossroads none,  
a split in destiny with no will to be one.  

We walk parallel:  
Together, but not together,  
Forever, yet never ever.  

A constant endeavour to breakthrough,  
to cross paths with someone,  
to delve into roads of others,  
But to no avail.  

We walk parallel,  
Following fate's frail trail.  

---

### III. The Replica

How do I tell my dreams that you left a long time ago?  
That the one who comes in them is just a creation of my own,  
A mere replica of what I wanted us to be,  
A mere replica of what I wanted to see.  

How do I tell that the perfect dream wasn't perfect,  
That from a long time it was wrecked?  

How do I tell my dreams that you left a long time ago?  
That the illusions of your existence were just an attempt  
of a heart in despair,  
A mere try to attain frail relief,  
A mere try to hold on and believe.  

How do I tell that everything was fiction,  
Something that I made, imagined with conviction.  

---

# ACT II: THE TWILIGHT PURGATORY (12:00 TO 05:00)

> *When the day's obligations surrender to the dark, the bedroom ceiling becomes an interrogation room. Here, time loses its momentum, and memory tests how much reality you can trade for an illusion.*

---

### IV. The Stationary Position

One night alone,  
Drenched in the thoughts of a solitary mind,  
Learning the lessons from his own life,  
Stationary position from twelve to five:  
One arm on forehead, other right beside.  

One night alone,  
Led by the ocean of possibilities  
That would've made living a lesser liability,  
The choices that were under his ability,  
The chances that blew up for his insanity.  

One night alone,  
Having a run-through of the passing day,  
Rewriting the talks that took place,  
Recalling that one special gaze in different ways,  
from that special face.  

One night alone,  
With imagination soaring high and persistent,  
Breaking free into fantasy existence,  
A fascinating alternative to daily perseverance,  
A remedy for feelings to find permanence.  

One night alone, again:  
To repeat the previous night,  
With new questions and problems to fight,  
For letting go of the regrets inside,  
For another twelve-to-five fantasy ride.  

---

### V. The Enigma of Sleep

Dreams...  
Interesting, ain't they?  

Makes us smile,  
Makes us tremble,  
Makes us shed tears array.  

A reflection of what we want,  
Or what we fear.  
A meeting with some unreal memories,  
Some we despise, some we hold dear.  

A world of its own,  
Rebellion against the laws of nature.  
A place to face some faces,  
Or some realistic caricature.  

A mirror of our emotions,  
A deep pathway to a buried soul:  
Ain't it an interesting puzzle?  
Never does it become a whole!  

---

### VI. These Days

These days...  

These days I often look at the clouds,  
Gaze towards the sky and ask:  
Could you be a little more loud?  

These days,  
I am running out of words,  
So I ask the stars for their thoughts,  
If they could give me lots.  

These days,  
I think about you a little bit more,  
More than the bees thought of flowers,  
These days, you're on my mind for hours.  

These days,  
I care less about everything,  
I tend to be a little lost soul,  
And try to accept pieces rather than the whole.  

These days,  
The flavours seem so bland,  
The hues don't merge together,  
And the hymns don't rhyme sometimes...  

These days, I feel a little less me.  

---

### VII. The Stranger's Hypothesis

If we cross roads again someday,  
I wish we would be strangers by then,  
To see if my heart will beat for you,  
All over again.  

If we face each other in the future,  
Let's just be unknown,  
And let our memories be gone.  

If we hold hands once more,  
Let's not have the spark we used to,  
And just put on a casual attitude.  

If we ever fall again,  
Let's not fall for each other this time,  
Let's just pick a different rhyme.  

If only this was possible:  
Would it be better or not?  
Would you still dominate my thoughts,  
Or would it be a different plot?  

---

# ACT III: THE WIDER HORIZON (FROM "I" TO "WE")

> *Suffering feels unique until you look out at the morning commute. The realization arrives without fanfare: everyone around you is quietly navigating their own wreckage, wearing masks just thick enough to survive daylight.*

---

### VIII. The Lost Ones

In search of our destiny,  
In search of its flower path,  
We wander in the shadows,  
Calculating the aftermath.  

Aimlessly we look for the meanings  
Behind our long, tiring runs.  
How often do we realize  
That we are the lost ones?  

When was the last time we halted  
The flow of monotony,  
To ponder over our existence,  
To experience a gentle epiphany?  

Rare occurrences of reasoning  
Occur to those who run their conscience,  
A precious opportunity to find yourself  
Amongst the herd of lost kinds.  

Someday,  
The world will be clear,  
And our hearts will be whole.  
But for now,  
Everyone's a lost soul.  

---

### IX. The Broken Choir

We are a bunch of broken kids,  
Advising others to be strong.  
We are a bunch of off-tune notes,  
Still struggling to make a song.  

We may be smiling upfront,  
Hiding the chaos as we fall,  
But you'll see us cry our heart out  
Once you break through our wall.  

We are a bunch of masked men,  
We put on a fake face,  
A few unmask us to reveal  
The kid trapped in the devil's place.  

We are a bunch of lost souls,  
Looking for hope despite the tears,  
Like a wounded warrior  
Fighting till death despite the fears.  

We are looking for love from this world,  
We have waited, waited for so long:  
In the end, we are a bunch of broken kids  
Advising others to be strong!  

---

### X. The Distant Gaze

One day,  
When we would've walked a long distance,  
Will our roads cross over?  

When our appearance changes a bit,  
And the way we talk differs,  
Will we recognise each other?  

Maybe the memories will flash again,  
And the old feelings make a cameo.  
If the past conversation pops up in our head again,  
And the smiles strike our thoughts,  
Will we continue our thoughts further?  

Maybe we will forget it all,  
And meet as strangers,  
Or at least pretend to be unacquainted,  
And do away with any interaction:  
Shall we do it rather?  

Maybe the roads will diverge a lot,  
Not meeting at a crossroad ever.  
As we continue to walk along,  
Will we remember?  
Maybe as a faint flicker in the memory.  

That day, shall we look back or head straight?  

One day, when we would've walked a long distance....  

---

# ACT IV: THE FORGE OF AGENCY

> *Pity is a quiet trap. At some point, the letter must be signed, the laces tied, and the wheel taken. The stars do not descend to rescue anyone, they only witness the ones who decide to stand.*

---

### XI. The Unsent Letter

Hey,  
How're doing? Are you well?  
Maybe you're not going to tell.  

Well, things are messy here,  
But don't worry, it's still better than hell.  
What have I been doing?  
Not everything that I shall.  

I stop sometimes too,  
To think if I should continue.  
But I should, right? That's for the best.  
I should try hard before I rest.  

Am I making it hard for myself?  
Honestly, I don't know.  
The circle is small to ask for help,  
And even lesser number of places to go.  
Taking it easy ain't easy for me,  
And the road I took is slow:  
Slow and sad, mildly mad,  
Not very bad though.  

Why am I saying it here?  
Maybe to vent out the suffocating air,  
So I could breathe freely for a while,  
And tie up laces for the next mile.  

I do deserve this break, everyone does.  
Maybe that's what we need to do:  
Walk out of set patterns,  
Set aside preset moulds.  
Then, will a different story unfold?  

Okay, whatever. That doesn't matter.  
Not many things remain to shatter.  

Sorry for disturbing, but I may do it again.  
But until then,  
This is the end of this letter.  

---

### XII. Conscience in Percussion

Turned down my introversion,  
The last bit of conscience in percussion.  
Neck-and-neck battle with hesitation,  
And insignificant things in salvation.  

Down where I go,  
Deep there I see:  
Rogue waves conquer my sea of tranquility.  
A million positives, a billion negativity,  
Still determined to extend my infinity.  

Knocked over every toss,  
I still cling on to every loss.  
It's been my life: play and pause,  

An endless loop  
Of chaos and cause!  

---

### XIII. The Falling Stars

Too many wishes to make that even these fallin' stars fall short,  
Too many things to accomplish in this one lifetime you got.  

The sparkle of hope in the eyes of your family,  
The weight of not turning it into a tragedy,  
All of it has pushed you down on your knees,  
But you shouldn't be like this for long.  

What you have, you need to show it to them,  
Overcome yourself and carve out a gem,  
For you'll be the only one taking over your helm,  
You'll be the only one making you strong.  

All the hurdles that you jump over  
Will become your life's four-leaf clover,  
You just need to keep yourself sober  
Until the bad days are gone.  

Maybe you'll be exhausted, maybe you'll faint,  
Maybe you'll fill up your painting with the wrong paint,  
Even then, you'll pull out of your restraint,  
Clean up the stains and move on.  

Too many wishes to make,  
Too many things to accomplish.  
No stars will make it happen,  
You need to grant your own wish.  

---

# ACT V: THE RECONVERGENCE & THE SOUND OF STILLNESS

> *Peace does not mean forgetting the journey; it means walking back to the intersection that once broke you, looking at the stones, and discovering that they no longer hurt.*

---

### XIV. The Sound of Silence

While I sit in solitude,  
My mind echoes a phrase,  
Decorated by your silence  
And your soft smile, gracing your face.  

Yes, in the silence I hear you speak your heart,  
Not even a sound was leaked,  
Yet we conversed miles apart.  

You became the sound of my silence,  
The one residing in my little space,  
With whom I share my demons,  
The exit to my maze.  

In the midst of this quiet world,  
You're still the cure of my dementia;  
Your silent words were always heard,  
You are my source of euphoria.  

Let me be your lullaby too,  
The one which only you'll hear,  
The one which soothes you in your silence,  
The one to calm your fear.  

For once you'll hear me too,  
While you evade every other voice;  
Mine would be your sound of silence,  
Not by force, but by choice.  

We'll be each other's song,  
A song for us to hear,  
The notes of which we carry,  
The lyrics of which we wear.  

Let's lay down, eyes closed,  
Somewhere far from noise and nuisance,  
Let's not speak for a while,  
And hear our sound of silence!  

---

### XV. The Crossroad

I looked through the window,  
Familiar shadows were claiming the roads.  
These things which stand lifeless  
Carry our heavy loads:  

Loads: our shared memories,  
And the conversations that our hearts had,  
The things we did when we were together,  
Things we did, for which we are glad.  

The crossroad still exists,  
From where we used to part ways,  
And later came back to hold hands again,  
To head-start our days.  

But now I see new faces here,  
With some known souls as well.  
It's not us, all over again,  
But there remains our story to tell.  

I'll tell you what I heard here this time,  
When we hold hands again in another place.  
I'll tell you how our path gave way  
To other connected souls to have their space.  

Then, someday let's just come back here,  
Just to live those memories again,  
To once again claim the crossroads  
And hold hands as the night ends.  

---

# EPILOGUE & AUTHOR'S NOTE

> *"The road was never straight, nor was it meant to be easy. We carry our quiet hours, our broken notes, and our unspoken letters not as weight, but as proof that we walked, we felt, and we chose to continue."*
